How to play

Open your iPhone Duo like a laptop. One player draws the word on the inner screen. The guesser sits facing the cover screen and guesses out loud. Tap Got it when the guess is right, or Skip to try another word.

Common questions

The camera is blank. Allow camera access in Settings, then Guess My Doodle Duo.

Nothing shows on the cover screen. Fully unfold into laptop position. The game still works without it.

Can I play on a regular iPhone? Yes. The app switches to a single phone mode where the drawer sees a word card first.

Where do my saved pictures go? To your Photos library, only when you tap Save.
