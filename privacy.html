Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Guess My Doodle Duo does not collect, store on our servers, share, or sell any personal data. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. It does not connect to the internet.

Camera

The app uses the camera during play to show live pictures of the players and to capture short "Round moments." These pictures stay on your device. They are deleted when a game ends unless you choose to save them.

Photos

If you tap Save, the app adds the pictures or replay videos you picked to your photo library. The app can only add items. It cannot read your existing photos.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
